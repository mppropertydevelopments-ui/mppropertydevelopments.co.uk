Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 🍪 Cookie Policy →
Plain English summary This site collects minimal personal data. If you fill in the quote form, your details come to us directly so we can respond to your enquiry. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the site. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Who we are

MP Property Developments is a sole trader business based in Yorkshire. You can contact us at contact@mppropertydevelopments.co.uk or by calling 07764 706116.

What data we collect and why

  • Quote form submissions: When you submit an enquiry, we receive your name, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message. This is processed by Formspree and sent to us directly.
  • Website analytics (consent required): If you accept cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects anonymised data about how you use this website. This cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Advertising conversion tracking (consent required): If you accept cookies, Google Ads conversion tracking helps us measure whether visitors who clicked our adverts went on to contact us.

Legal basis for processing

  • Quote form data: Processed on the basis of legitimate interest — responding to your enquiry and taking pre-contractual steps at your request.
  • Analytics and advertising: Processed only with your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies and declining on your next visit.

Third-party services

  • Formspree — processes and forwards quote form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Analytics 4 — website analytics, active only with consent. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Ads — conversion tracking, active only with consent.
  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces. Google may log IP addresses as part of this service.
  • Google Tag Manager — manages when tracking tags fire. Does not itself collect personal data.

How long we keep your data

  • Quote form enquiries are kept for as long as necessary to manage your enquiry — no longer than two years from last contact.
  • If you become a customer, a separate agreement will cover any ongoing data processing.
  • Analytics data is retained in Google Analytics for 14 months.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent for cookies, and complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@mppropertydevelopments.co.uk.
